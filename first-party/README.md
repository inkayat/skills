# First-party skills

Original skills live here, one folder each. The former experimental minimal-change and faithful-editing candidates are preserved privately, not repackaged here.

| Skill | Entry point | Purpose | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| TLW job entry | `tlw/SKILL.md` | Read-and-apply handoff for one explicitly authorized job through an already-installed TLW. Adds no router, wrapper or lifecycle; cites TLW commit `8566b3b` | This repository installs or activates nothing; host commands are external and verified where used. A bounded one-card native pilot of the earlier `1d401c4` revision reached the human merge gate; no native trial of `8566b3b` |

A first-party skill belongs in `<name>/SKILL.md` with conventional name/description frontmatter, its actual supporting references and an explicit license/provenance declaration. Keep upstream copies under `upstream/`; do not edit them into local forks. Further authoring, behavioral evaluation or consumer activation needs its own authorized task.

The upstream updater never reads, traverses, rewrites or removes this directory. Its offline `verify` therefore does not validate or certify anything here; check frontmatter and source-relative paths separately.
